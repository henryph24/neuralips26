We diagnose normalization-induced routing collapse in mixture-of-experts adapters on time series foundation models, prove a mutual-information bound for it, and show that three architectural fixes (RR-MoA, SR-MoA, Residual-IA+) share one principle: route on the raw, pre-normalization signal. This yields 54/54 wins over the best fixed adapter (26 to 79% MSE improvement) and beats full fine-tuning by 12 to 79% under a strictly frozen backbone.
