We diagnose normalization-induced routing collapse in mixture-of-experts adapters on time series foundation models, prove a mutual-information bound for it, and show three architectural fixes (RR-MoA, SR-MoA, Residual-IA+) share one principle -- route on the raw, pre-normalization signal -- yielding 54/54 wins over the best fixed adapter (26-79% MSE) and beating full fine-tuning by 12-79% under a strictly frozen backbone.
