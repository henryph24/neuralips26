## Recommendations

**Mandatory fixes (factual errors):**

1. **§B.1 L1791 — MOMENT-small layer count.** The paper says MOMENT-small has **6 encoder layers**, not 8. Cross-reference with `feasibility/model.py` and the project CLAUDE.md note that "MOMENT-small hidden states are `(B, 64, 512)`" (dim 512 is correct; layer count 8 is wrong per the official MOMENT paper's Table 4 / Section 3.3). Correct to "6 layers, d=512, L=512". This is a single-character fix but matters for reproducibility.

2. **§2 L232 — Moirai-MoE "routes frequency bands".** This is the **opposite** of what the paper claims. Moirai-MoE explicitly rejects frequency-level routing as "too coarse-grained" and uses **token-level** routing where experts emerge from data. Rephrase to: "Moirai-MoE routes individual tokens to data-driven experts that automatically specialize across time series patterns".

3. **§1 L64 / §4 L392 — Moirai "LayerNorm only".** Moirai uses **RMSNorm** internally and **does** apply (non-learnable) instance normalization at input/output. The "LayerNorm-only, no RevIN" framing is wrong on both halves. A safer reframing: "Moirai uses RMSNorm internally and applies non-learnable per-instance scaling at I/O, distinct from MOMENT's learnable-affine RevIN within the encoder pipeline". This actually **strengthens** the paper's mechanism story (the failure mode requires per-instance μ,σ stripping inside the pre-encoder pipeline that the router sees, which RevIN does and Moirai's I/O scaling may not, depending on hook placement). Recommend a careful sentence-level rewrite plus a footnote in §4 setup explaining the distinction. **This is the most consequential citation issue** because the cross-backbone story (Moirai = "no collapse" because "no RevIN") rests on this. The paper's empirical Moirai results stand, but the **mechanistic attribution** ("absence of RevIN-style instance norm") needs nuance.

4. **§4 L390 — godahewa2021monash for Weather/Electricity.** Citation slip. The LTSF Weather (21-feature, 10-min Jena) and LTSF Electricity (321-channel hourly) are not from Monash. Re-attribute Weather to Wu et al. 2021 (Autoformer; introduced the 10-min Jena variant in their public codebase) or to the Wetterstation source. Re-attribute Electricity to UCI ML repository (e.g., wu2021autoformer or trindade2015electricity for the UCI source). Monash hosts daily/weekly aggregations that do not match the LTSF benchmark configuration the paper actually uses.

5. **§1 L66 — TimesFM as "pooling-then-projection".** TimesFM uses an autoregressive Residual-Block head, not pooling-then-projection. Either drop TimesFM from this enumeration, or broaden the umbrella to "lightweight regression heads (linear projection, MLP, or autoregressive)".

**Soft fixes:**

6. **§1 L66 — MOMENT "pooling-then-projection".** MOMENT's actual head is "flatten-then-project" over all N patches. Either rephrase the umbrella, or mention "concatenate-then-project" specifically when introducing MOMENT.

**No action needed:** Citations 1a, 1c, 1d, 2a, 3, 4a–c, 6b, 7, 8, 9a, 9b, 11, 12, 13, 14, 15a–c verified as supported.
