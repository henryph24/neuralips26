Pioneering time series foundation models (TSFMs) such as MOMENT, TimesFM, and TTM pair a frozen backbone with a task-specific head. Because downstream series mix heterogeneous regimes, the natural upgrade is a mixture-of-experts (MoE) head: a router over architecturally distinct experts, one per window. However, this upgrade fails on instance-normalized backbones: Reversible Instance Normalization (RevIN) and similar layers strip the per-window mean and variance the router needs, a failure we call *normalization-induced routing collapse*. Whenever joint training makes even one backbone transformer block trainable, routing entropy falls to $0.000$, meaning that every input is sent to a single expert and the rest are never activated. A 720-run sweep across standard MoE rescue mechanisms (load balancing, z-loss, entropy regularization, ReLU routing, expert-choice) recovers at most $10.9\%$ MSE ($2.7\times$ worse than our causal intervention), because the failure is in the router's input, not its optimization. We formalize this via a mutual-information decomposition; the resulting signal-ratio statistic, computable before training, predicts dataset vulnerability (Spearman $\rho = -0.88$, $p < 0.002$) and flags boundary cases. The diagnosis prescribes a minimal causal intervention: *Raw-Routed Mixture of Adapters* (RR-MoA) routes on the raw, pre-normalization input. RR-MoA sets a new SOTA for adapter-based TSFM transfer under a strictly frozen backbone: 54/54 wins on the primary backbone (26–79% MSE improvement over the best fixed adapter), strictly beats LoRA, TRACE, AdaMix, and full fine-tuning at $p < 0.001$, and generalizes across four further backbones and an imputation task. Eight causal controls including a vision-modality replication isolate the cause to instance normalization, and predict a *Frozen Paradox*: frozen RR-MoA beats full fine-tuning by 12–79%, dominated by the MoE pool's architectural advantage with a smaller within-architecture freeze effect ($\leq 13\%$) attributable to gradient co-adaptation (Proposition 1). Applying the same raw-signal principle at the expert level (Residual-IA$^+$) closes the gap with DLinear on 107/123 cells across six backbones $\times$ four horizons.
